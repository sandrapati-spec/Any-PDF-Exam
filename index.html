Welcome to Your Personal Exam Portal

Turn Any Question PDF into an Interactive Mock Test & Track Your Progress

This platform lets you convert any question-and-answer PDF or text into a real timed exam interface with 100% Vector & Text Diagram Recreation (circuits, phasors, graphs, Venn diagrams, geometry figures, and tables), organize your attempts into separate exam folders (like UIIC AO, GATE, ESE, TGPSC), and visualize your score improvement over time.

How to Use This Website Properly

Follow these simple steps to create error-free mock tests with full diagrams and track your marks.

1

Sign In to Sync Progress

Click Sign in with Google in the top bar so your exam sections and paper attempts sync across devices.

Works offline too!
2

Universal Diagram AI Prompt

Copy our strict AI prompt and upload your PDF to Gemini/ChatGPT. The AI recreates every circuit, graph, or figure using [SVG_START] or [DIAGRAM_START] code blocks!

Captures curves, circles & text labels
3

Organize in Exam Sections

Select a pre-existing exam section from the dropdown or choose + Create New Section... to type a custom name manually.

Multiple papers per section
4

Track Progression Graphs

Open Section Analytics to review isolated score progression lines and marks comparison charts for each exam.

Detailed answer key reviews

1. Exam Section & Paper Details

Select or create an exam section. You can attempt multiple papers under each section.

2. Convert Any PDF via Free AI

SVG + ASCII Mode

Upload your question PDF to Gemini or ChatGPT with this prompt. It instructs the AI to recreate every kind of diagram (circuits, curves, circles, Venn diagrams, phasors, graphs, and tables) with all text labels inside [SVG_START]...[SVG_END] or [DIAGRAM_START]...[DIAGRAM_END]!

3. Paste Strict Formatted Questions or Upload File

Automatically renders [SVG_START]...[SVG_END] vector graphics and [DIAGRAM_START]...[DIAGRAM_END] text schematics.

Supports Vector SVG Diagrams, ASCII Text Schematics, and Ctrl+V Image Paste.

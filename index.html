Welcome to Your Personal Exam Portal

Turn Any Question PDF into an Interactive Mock Test & Track Your Progress

This platform converts any question-and-answer PDF or text into a timed exam interface with MathJax LaTeX Equations (exponents, fractions, matrices, integrals) and Vector SVG Diagram Recreation (circuits, phasors, graphs, Venn diagrams), organizes attempts into exam folders (like UIIC AO, GATE, ESE, TGPSC), and visualizes your score progression over time.

How to Use This Website Properly

Follow these simple steps to create error-free mock tests with full math formulas, diagrams, and marks tracking.

1

Sign In to Sync Progress

Click Sign in with Google in the top bar so your exam sections and paper attempts sync across devices.

Works offline too!

Engineered LaTeX + SVG Prompt

Copy our engineered AI prompt in Step 2. It formats all powers, fractions, and roots in clean $...$ LaTeX and draws lightweight [SVG_START] diagrams!

Crystal-clear powers & fractions
3

Organize in Exam Sections

Select a pre-existing exam section from the dropdown or choose + Create New Section... to type a custom name manually.

Multiple papers per section
4

Track Progression Graphs

Open Section Analytics to review isolated score progression lines and marks comparison charts for each exam.
